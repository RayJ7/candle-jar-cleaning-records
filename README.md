# Candle jar cleaning model records

Eight selected candle-jar model/family records from five brands, with manufacturer cleaning routes, water placement, intended after-use and source dates.

## Files

- [candle-jar-cleaning-models.csv](candle-jar-cleaning-models.csv): the eight records; one model/family per row.
- [sources.csv](sources.csv): nine manufacturer documents, the sections read, checked dates and acquisition notes.

UTF-8, comma-delimited CSV with a header row and quoted fields where needed. Multiple values within a field use a vertical bar surrounded by spaces. Join source_ids to source_id for provenance. Source documents were checked **October 18, 2026**; this export was prepared October 21, 2026. The export date is not a new manufacturer review date.

## Reading the fields

after_use_status is exactly Allowed, Prohibited or Not stated. It applies only to intended_after_use, not every future use of the jar. status_with_scope keeps the essential qualifier in the same field: **Allowed (compatible refill block only)** is not permission to pour your own wax mixture. Not stated is neither approval nor refusal. Unknown details remain in unknowns.

For an unidentified model, use the base label or order record and ask the manufacturer; do not borrow another row's cleaning route. A clean jar is not automatically refillable. Availability, condition of a particular jar, food-contact suitability and historical refill compatibility are not established by these records. No physical cleaning or burn test was performed.

Method and reading context: [Match a candle jar to its maker's cleaning instructions](https://waxweigh.com/guides/how-to-get-wax-out-of-candle-jar).
